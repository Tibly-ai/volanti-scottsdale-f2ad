Hundred Dollar Hamburger

Pilots have a name for flying somewhere just to eat: the hundred dollar hamburger. Scottsdale Airport makes the trip easy, and the second floor of the Aviation Business Center makes it worth the fuel. Taxi in, walk up, take a table by the glass and watch the next departure roll out while the kitchen works.

Everything comes out of a scratch kitchen that leans on what is fresh that week, so the plate changes with the season. If you are short on time before the next leg, the bar seats are the quickest way in.

The Ritz-Carlton Paradise Valley, The Palmeraie Opening Party

Our team packed up the kitchen and headed south for the opening celebration of The Ritz-Carlton Paradise Valley, The Palmeraie. Passed bites, chilled rose and a long summer evening in the Valley.

Off-site nights like this one are a reminder of how much we enjoy cooking for a room full of people at once. If you are planning something of your own, our private events and jet catering teams work the same way.
