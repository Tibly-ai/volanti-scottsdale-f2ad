The Idea

The perfect
date night,
airside

Volanti offers a customized helicopter experience for two. Pair a three-course dinner and drinks at the restaurant with a flight around the valley to take in the views of the city. Larger helicopters are available for groups up to five.

The experience can be paired with a three-course lunch, brunch, or dinner. Have a larger group? We can create a custom flight package with multiple helicopters and flight plans.

No matter the occasion or your party size, Volanti can craft an experience to meet your needs and memories you'll never forget.

What's Included

Flight · Table · Credit

01

The Flight

A flight around the valley with a licensed pilot, departing from our private runway access at Scottsdale Airport. Choose a flight time that works best for you when you book.

02

$150 Food & Beverage Credit

A $150 credit toward your food and beverage for the visit. That lets you and your guests order from our full menu, choosing the items you actually want rather than a fixed plate.

03

A Three-Course Table

Pair the flight with a three-course brunch, lunch, or dinner from our scratch kitchen, served overlooking the runway and the McDowell Mountains. Brunch runs 9AM to 3PM Saturday and Sunday, lunch 11AM to 3PM Tuesday through Friday, dinner from 4:30PM Tuesday through Saturday.

04

Arrival & Check-In

Please arrive at least 15 minutes ahead of your flight reservation time and check in at the restaurant. That gives us time to get you situated and down to our private runway so your flight takes off on time. No security checkpoint, and covered parking sits directly across from the building.

Larger Groups & Custom Packages

Larger helicopters seat up to five. For bigger parties we build custom packages with multiple helicopters and multiple flight plans, timed around one shared table at the restaurant. Birthdays, proposals, client visits, anniversaries: tell us the occasion and we'll build the day around it.
