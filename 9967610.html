Before You Arrive

Three things worth knowing

01

Patio Seating

We do not take exclusive reservations for the patio. It is a high demand area with very limited space, so availability cannot be guaranteed in advance. Patio seating is limited to an hour and a half for parties of four or fewer, and two hours for parties of five or more. Add a patio preference to your reservation and we will do our best when you arrive. If nothing is open at your time, let the hostess know and we can put you on the patio wait list.

02

Finding Us

You do not need to be flying out, and there is no security checkpoint. Volanti sits on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center, which has direct access to the restaurant. Park in the large covered lot directly across from the building, shared with the City of Scottsdale Airport staff, our venue and United States Customs. Scottsdale Quarter is a six minute walk away.

03

Dining In Only

Our restaurant team is unable to accommodate to-go orders. Volanti runs a scratch kitchen that prioritizes fresh, seasonal offerings, and we keep that focus on the guests seated with us. If you are short on time, bar seats are available for a quick bite. A kids menu is on hand at brunch and dinner, and happy hour runs at the bar.
