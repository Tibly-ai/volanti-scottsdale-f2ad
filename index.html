The Room

Second floor,
runway side.

Volanti Scottsdale sits on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center, looking out over Scottsdale Airport and the McDowell Mountains. You do not need a boarding pass and there is no security checkpoint between the parking lot and your table. Walk in through the Business Center, take the stairs, and the runway is right there.

The kitchen is a scratch kitchen. Menus follow the season, which is why they change through the year, and why we cannot send plates out the door: everything is built for the room it is served in. For guests with a tight window, bar seats are the fastest way to eat well.

Wine Lockers

Our in-house sommelier helps you curate a selection stored on site for your exclusive use. Members get a locker and name plate, a discount on bottle purchases, priority on patio reservations, first access to events and wine dinners, after-hours offers, venue discounts, and limited-release wines and spirits.

Become a VIP

02

Helicopter Tour

A customized flight for two over the valley, paired with a three-course lunch, brunch or dinner and drinks. Larger helicopters seat groups up to five, and bigger parties can fly multiple aircraft on a custom flight plan. The package includes the flight plus a $150 credit toward food and beverage.

Plan the Flight

03

Events & Jet Catering

Private events in a room with a runway view, and catering prepared by the same scratch kitchen for departing aircraft. Tell us the date, the headcount and the departure time, and we will build the rest around it.
