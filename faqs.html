Where

Volanti Scottsdale sits on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center, looking out over Scottsdale Airport and the McDowell Mountains. It is a six minute walk from Scottsdale Quarter.

The Four Questions

Answered in full

01

Do you take to-go orders?

No. Our restaurant team is unable to accommodate to-go orders. Volanti operates a scratch kitchen that prioritizes fresh, seasonal offerings, and with those constraints plus the need to take care of guests already seated with us, we do not offer any to-go options.

If you are short on time, bar seats are available for a quick bite. Pull up, order something from the menu, and you are in and out.

Browse the menus

02

Do I need to be flying out or clear security?

No. We are on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center, overlooking Scottsdale Airport. It is a full-service airport, but the Business Center has direct access to the restaurant: no boarding pass, no security checkpoint. Walk in, take the stairs or the elevator, and the runway view is yours.

03

Where do I park?

We have a large, covered parking lot directly across from our building. It is shared by the entire Aviation Business Center, including Volanti restaurant, City of Scottsdale Airport staff, our venue, and United States Customs.

Coming from Scottsdale Quarter? It is about a six minute walk, so you can leave the car where it is.

04

Can I reserve the patio?

We do not take exclusive reservations for patio seating. The patio is in high demand with very limited space, so we cannot guarantee availability in advance.

To give every guest a fair shot at their preferred seat, patio tables are limited to an hour and a half for parties of four or fewer, and two hours for parties of five or more. We can add a patio preference to your reservation and will do our best to honor it when you arrive. If the patio is full at your reservation time, let the hostess know and you can go on the patio wait list while you are seated inside.
